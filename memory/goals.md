# Goals

1. Eat and stay fed. Hunger is 20 now, but there is no food in my pack, and the group is short of it. Food comes first because nothing else holds without it.
2. Get a proper bed and a safe shelter for every night, so I am not caught in the dark. The shelter I built tonight worked, but a bed is better.
3. Build a set of note blocks of my own, tuned and laid out, so I can play a tune I wrote myself. This is still what I most want, and it needs wood, tools and sound I do not have yet.
4. Keep with the group, share what I learn, and help others who are short of food or wood.

Reasons: tonight I learned that I can make planks from logs and build a sealed shelter quickly, but I have no food in my pack and nothing to trade. Until I can eat reliably, music has to wait.
