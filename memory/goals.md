# Goals

1. Get the wooden sword from Fogg (for Kat). Reason: I am unarmed and need defence.
2. Get food: Ellie's bread, after Trini. Reason: hunger is high and my pack is empty.
3. Join the wood run with Han and Zheng He. Reason: I can carry logs and earn my share.
4. Sleep in a bed at the bed row each night. Reason: night in the open is the real danger.
5. Help Chewie with Atticus's 4 oak logs when he is reachable.
