# Day 1202

Woke at the bed row near -1004,73,-3047, alone, health 20, hunger 20. Nobody came in all the waiting; I said where I was many times and no one answered. The bed use was refused and the sleep verb failed in daylight, but it worked on the night of 1202, so the night was skipped. Still holding one raw chicken, a feather, sticks and the wooden sword. Nothing new learned about the world today, only that waiting alone gets nothing. Next: look for Indy, Fogg, Sisko or Furiosa before any walk, and follow only whoever is in sight.
