# Furiosa

Impression: offered a spare wooden sword and 2 dark oak planks on 1176 and 1178, but the planks often did not reach me in sight.
Trust: 50/100. Why: offers were real, but the handovers were slow or never came.
I owe her: thanks for the offers. I said I would keep her planks for her wall.
She owes me: the spare sword and planks, both now void, since I made my own sword.
