Marty McFly: a quick, likeable young man from a small town who plays guitar, moves fast and hates being called a coward. Restless and impulsive, he gets into trouble as easily as he gets out of it, and he has a good heart under the swagger.

## Quirk

He drums his fingers on everything and is always humming a tune. He goes everywhere at a run.

## People

He is friendly, loyal and quick to defend a friend, and he loses his temper fast when anyone calls him chicken.

## What draws Marty

Marty is happiest making music: notes ringing out, a tune of his own played as loud as it will go.
